🌸 Inspiration Roleplay Gang Handbook

Official Gang Rules & Criminal Organization Standards

Version 3.0 Effective: 04/01/2026 Subject to Change

📖 Purpose

⌄

The Inspiration Roleplay Gang Handbook exists to keep gang roleplay fair, serious, balanced, and enjoyable for all players. Official gangs are expected to create storylines, conflict, business, tension, and community-driven roleplay instead of focusing only on winning fights.

Gang status is a privilege, not a right. Staff may remove official status, perks, benches, vehicles, MLOs, or whitelist access if a gang fails to meet community expectations.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

1. General Gang Expectations

⌄

All gangs are expected to maintain high-quality roleplay at all times. Members should value their lives, respect server rules, avoid excessive shoot-first behavior, and create meaningful stories with civilians, police, businesses, and rival organizations.

Gangs should act like criminal organizations, not deathmatch groups. Conflict should have buildup, motive, consequences, and roleplay progression.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

2. Official Gang Requirements

⌄

Official gangs must maintain active leadership, consistent activity, proper recruitment standards, and positive community involvement.

Staff may review gangs for activity, rule violations, leadership issues, poor RP standards, or abuse of gang perks. Inactivity or low standards may result in loss of whitelist status, MLOs, benches, custom vehicles, or other perks.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

3. Rule of 6

⌄

A criminal scenario may involve a maximum of 6 active participants per group.

This includes setup, execution, escape, lookouts, communications, scouts, interceptors, drivers, shooters, and anyone assisting in any meaningful way.

If you are involved in the situation, you count toward the 6. Being “only on radio,” “only watching,” or “only scouting” still counts if you are assisting the group.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

4. Criminal Scenario Rules

⌄

The Rule of 6 applies to robberies, heists, hostage situations, drug deals, weapons sales, kidnappings, convoy hits, prison breaks, ambushes, and gang operations.

No late joins. No hidden backup. No reinforcement waves. No extra members joining after the scenario begins. Once the group has committed to the scenario, the participant count is locked.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

5. Gang Conflict Rules

⌄

Gang conflict must be roleplay-driven. Valid conflict may include turf disputes, drug territory, disrespect, retaliation, extortion, business disputes, theft, betrayal, or storyline development.

Attacking gangs may bring a maximum of 6 attackers. All attackers must be present before the push begins. No reinforcements may join once started.

Defending gangs may only use players already present at the location when the attack begins. Members cannot rush in after hearing about it unless staff determines the balance clause applies.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

6. Turf Rules

⌄

Turf ownership does not grant permanent KOS rights. A gang must maintain presence, business, influence, and roleplay within its turf.

Turf disputes should involve negotiations, threats, taxation, protection rackets, meetings, intimidation, or other RP before escalating to violence.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

7. Official War Rules

⌄

Before war begins, both gangs must agree on war terms, objectives, limits, and victory conditions. A staff ticket must be opened so staff can oversee the war and ensure rules are followed.

War terms may include duration, turf involved, weapons allowed, participant limits, cooldowns, win conditions, and surrender terms.

No spawn camping, hospital camping, apartment camping, OOC harassment, or repeated targeting without RP progression.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

8. Alliance Rules

⌄

Gangs may have alliances, business agreements, intelligence sharing, and trade relationships.

Alliances may not be used to bypass the Rule of 6. Allied gangs may not join wars, stack numbers, or act as extra manpower in active conflicts.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

9. No Third-Party Involvement

⌄

Only the original parties involved may participate. No random players, outside gangs, friends, or uninvolved groups may interfere.

No helping one side, shooting both sides, third-party rescue attempts, or joining after the scenario begins.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

10. Downed = Out Rule

⌄

If you are downed, flat-backed, treated by EMS, or taken to the hospital, you are removed from the scenario permanently.

You may not return, rejoin, continue fighting, provide radio information, or participate in any way once removed.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

11. No Chain Fighting / Re-Engagement

⌄

Once a scenario ends, you must wait at least 30 minutes before re-engaging the same group.

This prevents revenge loops, endless shootouts, and chain fighting. Staff may extend cooldowns if a situation becomes excessive.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

12. Hostage & Rescue Rules

⌄

Hostage scenarios must stay realistic and fair. Maximum of 6 participants applies.

No late joins, mass rescue attempts, or wave reinforcements. Hostages must value life and may not intentionally throw the scenario.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

13. Kidnapping Rules

⌄

Kidnappings require valid RP reasoning such as debt collection, retaliation, information gathering, business disputes, or gang conflict.

Random kidnapping, OOC targeting, harassment, or removing someone from RP without purpose is not allowed.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

14. False Flagging Rules

⌄

False flagging may be used to create conflict, tension, investigations, and storyline development.

Allowed actions include wearing another gang’s full outfit, using vehicles associated with another gang, and committing crimes to draw attention toward them.

Not allowed: claiming to be a specific person, using OOC knowledge, repeatedly targeting the same gang without progression, or using false flagging only to create reports or confusion.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

15. KOS Guidelines

⌄

KOS is heavily restricted and must always be justified by in-character roleplay.

KOS is not permanent, global, or automatic. It only applies to involved individuals or active conflicts and cannot be used as an excuse for RDM.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

16. Recruitment Standards

⌄

Recruitment should be realistic and roleplayed. No mass recruiting, instant trust, instant high ranks, or OOC pressure.

Prospects should earn trust, prove loyalty, learn rules, and understand gang expectations before becoming full members.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

17. Safe Zones

⌄

Protected areas include hospitals, MRPD lobby, courthouse, spawn areas, character creation areas, and staff event zones.

No shootouts, kidnappings, executions, ambushes, or gang conflict should occur in safe zones unless staff-approved.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

18. Gang Assets & Property

⌄

Gang assets include MLOs, benches, stashes, custom vehicles, businesses, and special perks.

These are privileges tied to activity, good conduct, and roleplay quality. Staff may remove assets for abuse, inactivity, or repeated rule violations.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

19. Law Enforcement Interaction

⌄

Police are players, not NPCs. Gangs should create investigative RP instead of constant shootouts.

Good gang RP includes front businesses, money laundering, informants, smuggling, corruption arcs, fake identities, and hidden operations.

Avoid cop baiting, unrealistic police attacks, and repetitive shootouts with no story.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

20. Investigation & Raid RP

⌄

Police investigations and raids are valid RP. Gangs may hide evidence, relocate assets, counter-investigate, and create legal or criminal responses.

Gangs may not meta investigations, use OOC information, log off to avoid consequences, or move assets after learning something OOC.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

21. Motorcycle & Bike Usage Rules

⌄

Motorcycles are allowed for gang operations, drug trafficking, scouting, transportation, store robberies, and smaller criminal scenarios.

Major heists may not use motorcycles as the primary getaway vehicle unless staff-approved. Major heists include Pacific Standard, vault robberies, yacht heists, cargo ship heists, major event robberies, or any operation requiring large police response.

No rat routes, mountain abuse, impossible terrain, extreme stunt jumps, repeated bike-only escape paths, or hidden bike chains designed to remove police counterplay.

Motorcycles have advantages and disadvantages. Choosing a bike means accepting the risk of less protection, easier ejection, and greater injury risk.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

22. Roleplay Standards

⌄

Roleplay comes before winning. Violence should be a tool, not the default answer.

Gangs are encouraged to use negotiation, extortion, protection rackets, intimidation, business agreements, diplomacy, blackmail, and long-term storylines.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

23. Staff Discretion

⌄

Staff have final authority on intent, fairness, rule abuse, loopholes, and scenario balance.

Staff may issue warnings, strikes, asset removal, suspensions, or gang disbandment.

The spirit of the rule always outweighs the wording of the rule.

Staff Reminder: Rule intent matters. Avoid loopholes and keep RP fair for everyone involved.

⌄

By participating in gang roleplay, all members acknowledge that they understand these rules and agree to follow them. Gang leaders are responsible for ensuring their members know the handbook.
